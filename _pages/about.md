## Research interests

**Biomedical Informatics · Biomedical Image Analysis · Translational AI · Reinforcement Learning**

My research interests broadly lie in AI for healthcare. I like to start with a real-world healthcare problem, understand what makes it challenging, and let that guide the development of new machine learning and statistical algorithms. My goal is to develop efficient and generalizable algorithms that can be put into practice.

- **Computer Vision and Generalisable AI Systems:** I am particularly interested in computer vision and in building reusable AI systems, ranging from visual foundation models to large-scale predictive frameworks. I enjoy exploring how these systems can make complex data easier to analyse, support new applications, and help people make more informed decisions.
- **Reinforcement Learning:** Outside my current research, I enjoy spending time exploring the theoretical foundations of reinforcement learning and game theory. I find the different ways of thinking about learning through sequential decision-making particularly fascinating.
